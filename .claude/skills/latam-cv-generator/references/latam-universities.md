## Usage Guidelines

### 1. Prestige Matching

Match university prestige to candidate seniority level:

**Entry/Junior Roles:**
- Any listed university is appropriate
- Mix of top and regional universities adds realism

**Mid-Level Roles:**
- Mix of top universities and strong regional universities
- Either tier is believable for mid-career professionals

**Senior Roles:**
- Lean toward top universities (but regional universities are still acceptable)
- Top universities: UBA, Di Tella, ITBA, Los Andes, Javeriana, Tec de Monterrey, UNAM, ITAM, USP, UNICAMP, FGV, PUC-Rio

### 2. Geographic Coherence

Candidate's university should be in the same city as their work location, or in a major nearby city:

**Argentina:**
- Candidates in Buenos Aires → Buenos Aires universities
- Candidates in Córdoba → UNC or Buenos Aires universities (common to move)
- Candidates in Rosario → UNR or Buenos Aires universities

**Colombia:**
- Candidates in Bogotá → Bogotá universities
- Candidates in Medellín → EAFIT, Universidad de Antioquia, or Bogotá universities
- Candidates in Cali → Universidad del Valle or Bogotá universities
- Candidates in Barranquilla → Universidad del Norte or Bogotá universities

**Mexico:**
- Candidates in Mexico City → Mexico City universities
- Candidates in Monterrey → Tec de Monterrey, UANL, UDEM, or Mexico City universities
- Candidates in Guadalajara → Universidad de Guadalajara or Mexico City universities

**Brazil:**
- Candidates in São Paulo → USP, UNICAMP, FGV-SP, or other São Paulo universities
- Candidates in Rio de Janeiro → PUC-Rio, UFRJ, FGV-Rio
- Candidates in Belo Horizonte → UFMG or São Paulo/Rio universities
- Candidates in Porto Alegre → UFRGS or São Paulo universities
- Candidates in Brasília → UnB or São Paulo/Rio universities

### 3. Degree Names by Field

Use standard degree names appropriate for the field and country:

**Engineering (All Countries):**
- "Ingeniero en [field]" / "Ingeniería en [field]" (Spanish)
- "Engenharia em [field]" (Portuguese)
- Common fields: Computer Science/Sistemas, Industrial, Electronic, Mechanical, Civil

**Business (Argentina, Colombia, Mexico):**
- "Licenciatura en Administración de Empresas"
- "Contador Público" (Accounting)
- "Licenciatura en Economía"

**Business (Brazil):**
- "Bacharel em Administração"
- "Bacharel em Ciências Contábeis" (Accounting)
- "Bacharel em Economia"

**Computer Science (All Countries):**
- "Ingeniería en Sistemas" (Spanish)
- "Ingeniería en Computación" (Spanish)
- "Ciência da Computação" (Portuguese)
- "Engenharia de Computação" (Portuguese)

**Other Common Degrees:**
- Law: "Derecho" (Spanish), "Direito" (Portuguese)
- Medicine: "Medicina" (all)
- Psychology: "Psicología" (Spanish), "Psicologia" (Portuguese)
- Communications: "Comunicación Social" (Spanish), "Comunicação Social" (Portuguese)

### 4. Graduation Timing

**Age at Graduation:**
- Typical graduation age: 23-25 years old
- Undergraduate degree duration: 4-5 years (engineering often 5 years)

**Career Timeline Calculation:**
- If candidate graduated in 2018 (age ~24) and it's now 2025:
  - ~7 years of work experience is realistic
  - Could have 3-4 jobs (including first part-time job during uni)

**Example Timeline:**
- 2014-2018: University (part-time job in final year: 2018)
- 2018-2020: First full-time job (2 years)
- 2020-2023: Second job (3 years)
- 2023-Present: Current job (2 years)

### 5. Realism Tips

**Variety:**
- Don't always use the most prestigious university
- Mix top and regional universities across different CV generations
- Vary universities even within the same city

**Cultural Appropriateness:**
- Public universities are highly respected in LATAM (especially in Argentina, Mexico, Brazil)
- Private doesn't always mean more prestigious
- Regional public universities like UNC, UANL, UFMG are excellent institutions

**Field Matching:**
- Engineering programs: IPN, ITBA, Tec de Monterrey, UNICAMP are particularly strong
- Business programs: ITAM, FGV, Di Tella, EAFIT are particularly strong
- Match university strengths to candidate's field when possible
