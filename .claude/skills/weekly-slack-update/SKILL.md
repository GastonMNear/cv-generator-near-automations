# End-of-week Slack update to Andrés

A Friday Slack message to **Andrés only** that wraps up where each workstream stands. It
closes the loops from the week, so the Monday 1:1 (`andres-1on1-doc`) can go to new
topics. When Andrés needs to decide something, this message is where he gets asked,
explicitly.

**Read [references/examples.md](references/examples.md) before drafting.** Gaston's sent
messages define the format, and that format is narrative status per workstream, not a
metrics report.

## Workflow

1. **Read last week's update** in `Near Work Partner/logs/weekly-updates/` and the latest
   1:1 entry in `logs/andres-1on1/`. Every item that was "in progress", "launching
   Monday", "finish by Tuesday" or raised in the 1:1 needs a closing line this week: done,
   still moving and why, or dropped. Closing loops is the main point of the message.
2. **Gather the week** (follow `obsidian-wiki`, `wiki/hot.md` first): meetings since the
   last update (TLDR and Action Items), tasks done or moved in `wiki/tasks/` + `done/`,
   `logs/`, and skills or routines shipped. Suggest `fathom-task-sync` if meetings look
   missing.
3. **Group by workstream** (e.g. Clay maintenance, onboarding, an experiment, cold
   calling, Smartlead/Lila), not by meeting. Merge related tasks into one item. Aim for
   about 5–8 items and drop anything Andrés doesn't need.
4. **Find the decisions.** Anything where Gaston needs Andrés's OK, an owner call, budget
   or a tradeoff becomes a "need your call" item, and the opening line counts them. If you
   aren't sure whether something is Gaston's call or Andrés's, ask Gaston.
5. **Draft**, then ask Gaston what's missing that isn't in the vault. Flag any
   unconfirmed statuses below the draft, not inside it.
6. **Save** Gaston's final sent version to
   `Near Work Partner/logs/weekly-updates/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (the Friday date). If he rewrote
   a lot, add the lesson to `references/examples.md`.

## Format

- **Opening line:** "Hey Andrés, quick wrap-up of where things stand this week." plus the
  decision count with pointers ("One decision needed from you (#6), everything else is
  just closing the loops."). If there are none, say so.
- **Numbered items**, each with a header in the form `Topic → Owner (status)`. Statuses are
  plain words: done, in progress, launching Monday, starts next week, need your call.
- **Body:** 2–4 sentences in Gaston's first-person voice: what changed, what's pending,
  next step and timing. Use sub-bullets only for per-person or per-cause breakdowns.
- **Decision items:** the problem, the options, Gaston's recommendation, then a yes/no ask
  ("If you're ok with it, I can hand that over to Ivan.").
- **Numbers** only where they explain something (a pickup rate, an audit finding). There's
  no metrics block, because the huddle deck and dashboards carry the scorecard.
- **Close:** "Let me know if everything's clear!"
- No vault links, Fathom timestamps or campaign IDs. Keep hearsay and people/staffing
  rumors out, and mention them to Gaston separately.
