"EXP0XX - short name:"**, then one or two sharp sentences with the key insight and the
decision, then "Readout here." linked to the Doc. Numbers only where they carry the point.

## Sent by Gaston, Oct 13 (reference format)

Hey team! Here is the Email experiments weekly update
• **EXP008A - Personalized follow-ups (Emails 2–3):** no lift from the personalized follow-ups yet. Still collecting data, decision at 2,500 leads. Readout here.
• **EXP008B - Longer sequence (Emails 4–7):** the extra emails aren't adding much so far (aprox same positive replies in total as just email 3 alone), although they did generate 1 extra call booked that we wouldn't have booked without the longer sequence. Still running, decision at full sample. Readout here.
• **EXP009 - CEO multi-role savings message:** I think we can stop this test. 0 positive replies from 758 CEOs, the list is too small to reach a full sample but these leads are not generating positive replies or calls booked so far. Readout here.
• **EXP011 - Adding the CEO in HM positions:** early signs show it brings in extra opportunities. Still early in the sample, but results are promising, keep running. Readout here.
• **EXP012 - Copy by role group (Engineering, Sales/GTM, Marketing):** all three are tracking close to baseline. Sales/GTM reaches full sample next week. However, there doesn't seem to be a significant outperformance. Next step would be to replace this experiment with department head specific copy. Readout here.
