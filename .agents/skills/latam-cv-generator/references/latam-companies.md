## Usage Guidelines

### 1. Industry Alignment

Choose companies from industries relevant to the job description:

**For Tech Roles:**
- Prefer: Globant, Mercado Libre, Nubank, IBM, Oracle, Accenture, Google, Microsoft
- Avoid: Pure manufacturing or retail companies unless role is tech-focused

**For Business/Operations Roles:**
- Good options: Consulting firms (Deloitte, PwC), Banks (Santander, BBVA, Itaú), Retail (Walmart, Falabella), Manufacturing (automotive companies)

**For Marketing/Sales Roles:**
- Good options: Consumer goods (Coca-Cola FEMSA, Unilever, Nestlé), Retail, Telecom, Tech companies

**For Engineering Roles (Non-Software):**
- Good options: Automotive (VW, GM, Ford), Manufacturing (Bosch, 3M), Energy (Petrobras, Ecopetrol), Telecom

### 3. Company Variety

**Avoid repetition:**
- Don't use the same company repeatedly across different CV generations
- Rotate between multinationals and strong regional companies
- Mix tech, finance, consulting, and industry companies

### 4. Title Appropriateness

Ensure job titles make sense for the company type:

**Good matches:**
- Software Engineer at Globant, Mercado Libre, Accenture, IBM ✓
- Financial Analyst at Santander, BBVA, Deloitte, PwC ✓
- Manufacturing Engineer at VW, Ford, Bosch ✓
- Marketing Manager at Coca-Cola FEMSA, Unilever, Nestlé ✓

**Bad matches:**
- Software Engineer at Coca-Cola FEMSA (retail/beverage company) ✗
- Manufacturing Engineer at McKinsey (consulting) ✗
- Petroleum Engineer at Magazine Luiza (retail) ✗

**Acceptable edge cases:**
- Software Engineer at banks (many have large tech teams) ✓
- Data Analyst at retail companies (e-commerce/analytics teams) ✓
- Operations Manager at almost any large company ✓

### 5. Realism Through Mixing

**Multinational vs. Regional:**
- Senior roles: More likely at multinationals (Google, McKinsey, Deloitte)
- Mid-level roles: Mix of multinationals and strong regional (Bancolombia, Grupo Éxito)
- Junior/Entry roles: Can start at any large company

**Remote for US Companies:**
- Some roles can be "remote for [US company]" especially in tech
- Examples: Remote Software Engineer for a US startup (but company should still have >1k employees)
- Use this sparingly to add variety

### 6. Employee Count Reminder

**CRITICAL:** The CV should NEVER mention the employee count. This information is for your reference only to ensure you select companies with >1,000 employees (for current role) or the right size range (for previous roles), but it should NOT appear in the generated CV.
