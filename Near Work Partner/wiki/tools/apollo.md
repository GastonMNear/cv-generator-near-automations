# Apollo

**Contact-coverage expansion layer.** Fills the gap when Clay and the AI researcher don't find the right person at a company.

## Why It Matters

Clay/AI-researcher miss the right contact on many open-job companies. Apollo finds those contacts so Near can actually reach relevant accounts — especially in **No-HM** and open-jobs campaigns where the company is relevant but the person isn't obvious.

**Estimated impact: ~3,000 additional leads/month.** Current project: expanding Apollo coverage for open jobs. See [[context/outbound-engine]].

## Notes

- Sits alongside Clay enrichment — Apollo is the fallback contact source, not the primary database.
- Weigh enrichment cost vs. expected opportunity value before pulling.
