# HireWithNear

Nearshore staffing and recruiting company. Helps U.S. companies hire remote talent from Latin America.

## What We Do

360 staffing and recruiting partner — source, vet, present, hire, onboard, pay, and retain LatAm professionals. Covers staffing, direct hire, payroll, benefits, compliance, and on-the-ground nearshore support.

**Core pitch:** strong English-speaking LatAm talent in aligned U.S. time zones, faster and cheaper than hiring in the U.S.

**Headline claims (use in outreach):**
- Shortlists in under 5 days; hire in ~3 weeks (under 21 days)
- 30–70% cost savings vs. U.S. hires (~$35k–$55k/yr per hire)
- 3,500+ placed, $150M+ saved, 45,000+ pre-vetted candidates, 97% placement rate

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

U.S. companies needing to scale teams fast, cut hiring costs, or fill hard-to-hire roles without sacrificing quality. Strong fits: startups, SaaS, agencies, finance/accounting, tech, healthcare, real estate, logistics, customer support — generally remote-friendly businesses.

## Roles Covered

Sales · Customer Support · Marketing · Finance & Accounting · Software Engineering · Data & AI · Design · Operations · Admin/VA · Legal Support · Executive Support. (8 departments, 70+ roles, entry-level to C-suite.)

## Business Model

Two offerings:
- **Recruiting / direct hire** — client hires the candidate directly, pays Near a success-based placement fee.
- **Staffing** — Near owns payroll and employment infrastructure; client manages the person's day-to-day work.
