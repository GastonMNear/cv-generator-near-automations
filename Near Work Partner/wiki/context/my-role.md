# Gaston's Role

**GTM Engineer / Sales Operations & Outbound Lead Gen.** Sits at the intersection of sales strategy, outbound infrastructure, and technical execution. Not a closing AE — owns and improves the systems that generate pipeline (the top-of-funnel engine) and supports the team with the tooling and workflows that make outreach work at scale.

The one-line version: **Gaston helps run and improve Near's outbound engine.** See [[context/outbound-engine]] for the full system.

## What He Owns

- **Cold email infrastructure, end to end** — target audience definition → lead lists → enrichment → sequencing → deliverability → campaign QA. Email is the broad-coverage layer: it touches almost every opportunity first, captures high-intent demand, and feeds LinkedIn and calling with prioritized leads.
- **Clay lead intelligence layer** — builds/maintains lead databases and enrichment workflows; DMU (Decision Making Unit) targeting, conditional routing formulas, firmographic filters, exclusion lists. See [[tools/clay]].
- **Smartlead sending infrastructure** — inboxes, domains, campaign setup, sending capacity, deliverability. See [[tools/smartlead]].
- **Campaign strategy** — decides which campaigns are active / paused / tested / expanded; designs cross-channel sequencing.
- **Outbound reporting** — owns/contributes heavily to the Outbound Command Center.
- **LinkedIn + cold calling support** — strategic and lead-gen oriented (generating/qualifying interest, feeding leads forward), not day-to-day manual sending or closing.

## How He Works

Operates fluidly across strategy and technical execution — rare. In a single day he'll go from defining ICP criteria → writing a Clay formula → drafting email copy → debugging a Make.com webhook. Prefers concise, copy-paste-ready outputs. Favors verified information over general knowledge. Brief and direct.

## Daily Workflow

Tactical days: clearing/assigning pending replies, connecting Smartlead inboxes/domains, pulling and enriching Clay lists, checking where contact coverage is missing, updating tracking sheets / the Command Center.

Strategic days: testing Apollo coverage, expanding LinkedIn, designing cross-channel sequencing, deciding which campaigns get more capacity.

Recurring daily checks: campaign performance, email volume vs. capacity, replies, pending responses, handoffs.

## Weekly Workflow

Review calls booked, reply volume, campaign health, capacity, experiment results, bottlenecks. Decide whether to launch a new test, continue an active one, pause, or use fallback campaigns so sending capacity isn't wasted. **Recurring principle: don't expand capacity just to send more — only if added volume goes to high-intent opportunities.**

## KPIs / Metrics

Primary: **calls booked**, emails sent, email capacity used, booking rate, reply rate, positive reply rate, bounce rate, leads added, new leads contacted, total leads contacted.
LinkedIn: connection requests sent, acceptance rate, replies, positive replies, calls booked per account, account-health/restriction flags.
Cost: Clay credits, inbox cost, domain cost, Smartlead cost, **cost per lead**, **cost per call booked**.

## What Success Looks Like

A healthier, more scalable outbound engine: more high-intent opportunities identified, more of the right people contacted, more calls booked, email capacity used efficiently, LinkedIn + cold calling support improved, and **repeatable workflows other teammates can run** — plus better visibility into what's working, why, and what to scale next.
