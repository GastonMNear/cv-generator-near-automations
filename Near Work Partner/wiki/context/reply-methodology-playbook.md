# Positive-Reply Handling Playbook

How we answer every positive reply from now on. Two rules drive everything:

1. **Speed** — a positive reply is peak intent. We treat the clock like an inbound demo request.
2. **Proof in every reply** — every positive reply gets an **AI CV** of a real past placement, whether or not they asked for a candidate.

---

## The Logic (why)

A positive reply to our cold email is the same moment as an inbound form fill: the prospect has decided we're worth a conversation *right now*. That intent decays fast.

- Respond within **5 min → 21x more likely to qualify** than at 30 min. After 5 min, qualifying odds drop **80%**; after 10 min, odds of reaching them at all drop **400%**.
- The **first vendor into a real conversation wins 35–50%** of competitive deals — they set the evaluation criteria.
- Only **7% of companies** respond within 5 minutes. Speed is a competitive edge sitting in plain sight, and **58% of buyers say response time is a critical factor** in choosing a vendor — it signals competence.

Source: MIT/InsideSales, Drift, HBR (avg B2B first response = **42 hours**). We're not competing on price alone — we're competing on being first and looking sharp.

**Why the AI CV on every reply:** the fastest way to prove caliber is to show a candidate. Attaching an AI CV to show the caliber of talent we can provide on every positive reply turns an abstract pitch into concrete proof in the first exchange, and gives the prospect a reason to book. It also lets us reply *fast with substance* instead of a hollow "thanks, let's chat."

---

## The Standard: Two-Email Flow

Every positive reply gets **two emails**, not one:

| Email | Timing (business hours) | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **E1 — Acknowledge** | **< 5 min** | Instant, warm receipt. Answer any direct question and provide the information requested by the lead (if any) + set the expectation that a candidate is coming. Buys time to build E2. |
| **E2 — Substance + AI CV** | **10–15 min after reply (hard cap 20 min)** | An AI CV of a relevant past placement + a booking CTA. |

Never let a positive reply sit longer than **20 minutes** during business hours. Overnight replies are the **#1 task at start of day** — cleared before anything else.

## Plays by Reply Type

Use our existing tags. All timings are business hours.

### 1. Interested / asked for a candidate or CV
- **E1 (<5 min):** "Amazing - let me pull a strong candidate from our database, I'll be right back with you in a few minutes."
- **E2 (10–15 min):** AI CV of a relevant 'past placement' (never passing it as a real candidate, but as an example of the caliber of talent we can provide) + booking CTA.
- If we're able to reply directly with the CV in <5 mins, then E2 is not needed.

### 2. Info request only (fee, process, timezones, guarantee)
- **E1 (<5 min):** Answer the question **directly** — do NOT say "let me check my database" for a fee/process question, it reads wrong. Just answer.
- **E2 (10–15 min):** Proactive AI CV as a second email — *"Since you're evaluating, here's a CV from one of our recent placements so you can see the caliber of talent we'd bring."* This is the new behavior: the CV goes even to people who only asked a question.
- This lets us reply fast (the CV generation will usually take longer than 5 min), but also have the CV impact the conversation while the lead is still hot. No need to wait for them to ask for it.

### 3. Meeting request - NO CV needed in this case
- **E1 - Priority = get the meeting booked**, fastest of all. Send the booking link / confirm a time in E1 (<5 min). No CV needed in this case.

## How to Actually Do It (operational)

**Owner:** Hector runs reply handling. First action every morning = clear overnight positive replies before any other work.

1. **Watch the inbox live during business hours.** A positive reply is an interrupt, not a queue item. Treat it like a ringing phone.
2. **Fire E1 immediately** from the acknowledgement template for the matching reply type (templates below). Under 5 minutes ideally, but the harder target is **under 10 minutes** for E1.
3. **Generate the AI CV** using the resume generator, matched to the role, industry and job description of the real job opening.
4. **Send E2** with the CV + answer + CTA within 10–15 min of the original reply.
5. **Log it** — name, lead email, subject — so response time is measurable per reply. Pending positive replies are the priority. If there's another reply incoming, first answer that and then create a new row in the log.
6. **Off-hours:** it's fine for a reply to sit overnight, but it is the first thing handled at start of day.

### Response-time targets (SLA)
| Tier | Target | Hard cap |
|---|---|---|
| E1 acknowledge | < 5 min | 10 min |
| E2 full reply + AI CV | 10–15 min | 30 min |
| Overnight replies | Handled first at start of day | — |

### Measurement
- **Daily + weekly** positive-reply response-time report (daily catches anomalies fast enough to react; weekly for the trend).

## What we are NOT doing
- **No** single slow "perfect" reply. Speed with the two-email split beats one delayed email.
- **No** 50/50 acknowledge A/B split test — we're betting on the two-email flow for everyone.
- **No** "let me check my database" in response to a pure fee/process question — answer it directly, then follow with the CV.
