# KPI Deck Prep — Statistical Sample Rule

Pre-meeting prep, roughly an hour before the outbound leadership review, finishing the weekly KPI
deck. **Not** the leadership meeting itself.

## TLDR
- **The week's numbers were bad:** 9 booked calls against a 30+ target, down from 18–19. LinkedIn
  collapsed — Samuel was OOO and Mairol only covered the Hayden and Franco accounts on Friday.
- **Email's bright spot is also its indictment:** 62 positive replies (up from 34) converted to only
  7 calls. Against the 20–25% target that should have been 12–13. **Converting that standing
  pipeline became the explicit focus of the week.**
- **The statistical sample rule was settled:** proving a winner on *calls booked* needs ~40,000
  leads and is unviable. Positive replies is the closest workable metric, but at 7,200 leads/arm
  that is still 6–8 weeks per test. Gaston owns both the written analysis and explaining it live —
  Daniel said outright he cannot present the statistics himself.
- **Experiment 009 survives.** Zero positive replies, but Gaston argued ~212 leads/arm is far too
  small a sample to kill on. Daniel agreed.
- **Weekend deliverability tests came back clean:** 50 inboxes, 100% primary placement. Gaston's
  theory for the earlier Microsoft spam result — an AI-written email variable rendered blank on a
  test lead, so a blank email got sent.

## Key Decisions
- Experiment success measured on **positive replies**, not calls booked (calls booked is
  statistically unviable at achievable lead volumes).
- The sampling tradeoff stays unresolved on the slides → moves to **Issues to Discuss**, with the
  analysis sent to the Slack channel ahead of the leadership call.
- Experiments capped at **30% of monthly lead volume** — 7,200 of 24,000 — leaving 16,800 baseline.
- Experiment 009 not killed despite zero replies; sample too small.
- Routing CTA promoted to **baseline** for No-HM; Experiment 008 moves onto that segment, capped.
- Leads at "improved connect timing" or "hiring in 3–6 months" count as **qualified long-term
  opportunities**, not unqualified.
