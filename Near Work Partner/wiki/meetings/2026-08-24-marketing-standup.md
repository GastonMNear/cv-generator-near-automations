# Marketing Stand Up — Attribution Mismatch & LinkedIn Retarget

## TLDR
- **Email booked 12 calls; the command center showed 11.** Gaston traced it to a real source
  mismatch this time — a double-booked company where a referral deal was created a day earlier, so
  the deal carries "Friend" as the source. The previous two weeks' gaps were *not* mismatches, just
  a dashboard not yet refreshed. **Nobody decided what to do with the misattributed deal.**
- **LinkedIn is changing its marketing-persona targeting mid-experiment.** Mairol/Felix move from
  broad "marketing heads" to **actively-hiring CMOs who are the hiring manager on the job post** —
  the CEO playbook applied to marketing. Split stays 50% proven / 50% test.
- **Andres challenged that change** as breaking the agreed three-week volume threshold. Felix argued
  it's a narrowing of the same group, not a new direction, and won the point for now — Andres
  deferred it to a 1:1.
- **The JustCall upgrade was effectively rejected.** Ivan needs more than 4 attempts per lead (his
  last booking converted on attempt 4), but the plan jump is ~$649/mo. Andres: raising spend on the
  channel with the worst cost-per-acquisition is a hard sell.
- **LinkedIn booked 4 calls combined** last week (2 from Samuel). Cold call is ramping — 1 booking
  the prior week, 2 this one.

## Key Decisions
- LinkedIn marketing-persona test **narrows to actively-hiring CMOs / job-post hiring managers**,
  starting this week, without waiting for the three-week threshold. Andres flagged but did not block.
- **No JustCall plan upgrade.** Andres judged the approval odds "very small" while cold call carries
  the worst CPA. Ivan's fallback: drop some purchased numbers to fund more attempts within budget.
- Sales Navigator higher tier is **still open** — Felix + Samuel meet the rep Wednesday to evaluate.
- Focus shifts toward the **hiring-manager segment** across channels — Daniel confirmed it is the
  best-converting segment on both LinkedIn and email, despite a poor pickup rate on HM dials.

## Context
The command-center-vs-actual gap has now appeared three weeks running, which is what prompted
Andres to dig in. Gaston's read is that only this week's is a true attribution failure. María Paz
confirmed the mechanism: booking through an AE's own link **auto-sets the source to email outreach**,
so anything else means the source was changed manually or a competing deal already existed. Her
guidance — when email outreach isn't showing as the source, check the deal log for a manual change
first.

On cold call, the constraint is structural rather than effort: JustCall's plan caps attempts per
lead, and Ivan's evidence is that leads convert on the fourth call while claiming they've never been
contacted. This is the same campaign-membership wall already tracked in
[[wiki/tasks/2026-08-21-justcall-api-move-reset-leads]], approached from the billing side.
