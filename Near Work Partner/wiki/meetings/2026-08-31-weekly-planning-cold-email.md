# Weekly Planning | Cold Email — Send Mix Diagnosis & the Remote-LATAM Copy Gap

> [!note] Language: Spanish. Short tactical stand-up (~13 min), not a full planning session.

## TLDR
- **Hector flagged zero replies; the cause was send mix, not copy or deliverability.** Of **4,279
  emails** sent that day, the vast majority were **follow-ups** — against a normal **~1,700 new
  contacts**. Gaston's read: the new sending schedules import fresh leads **later in the day**, so
  the morning ran on follow-ups only.
- **No lead-sourcing emergency.** Gaston verified **~300 leads already loaded but uncontacted**
  across Experiment 8B (~50) and Experiment 6 (44) among others, so new-lead sends should recover the
  same afternoon.
- **Hector found a real copy gap, and it's the highest-impact item on the call:** the sequences
  **never state the talent is remote LATAM**. Prospects assume US-based candidates and only discover
  otherwise when CVs arrive. He already patched his own resume-send text to say "remote candidates";
  the sequence copy needs the same fix.
- **"Experiment 5" gets renamed to "No Hiring Managers"** — the Experiment 8 No-HM test was folded
  into that table after Dani asked it move off Hiring Managers, so the number no longer describes
  the contents.
- **Follow-up copy is the leverage point**, since follow-ups are now most of the daily volume.
  Hector judges follow-up 1 acceptable and **2 and 3 weak**, and owes Gaston a doc today.

## Key Decisions
- **Do not treat the zero-reply morning as a copy or deliverability failure.** Attribute it to the
  follow-up-heavy send mix and wait for the afternoon new-lead sends before reacting.
- **The copy must state remote LATAM talent up front**, starting with email 1. Gaston confirmed he'd
  seen the same gap while reviewing the C-Asian campaign.
- **Rename the Clay table** from `Experiment 5` to `No Hiring Managers`.
- **Leave the "1–2 profiles" phrasing alone.** A prospect (Mohit Agarwal, C-Asian campaign) pushed
  back after being offered 1–2 profiles and receiving one. Gaston's judgment: we promised 1–2 and
  delivered 1, so nothing was over-promised — not worth changing.

all from the early-morning batch, and **1 positive reply landed during the call** itself.
