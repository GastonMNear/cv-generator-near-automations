## Key Decisions

**Book from the lead's link — but via our own link.** When a lead has a scheduling link, we don't
send ours (that hands the decision back to them). Offer a slot; if no reply by the next day, book
the slot ourselves and send a confirmation follow-up. A cancellation is acceptable.
> *"no uses su link para bukear directo, porque... no va a estar conectado con nuestro HubSpot... su link lo usamos solamente para ver tu calendar"* (don't book directly through their link — it isn't connected to our HubSpot; use it only to see availability)

**Forwarded-reply leads: pause the original, associate the new contact.** The forwarder already
did what we needed (connected us to the hiring manager), so he gets no further steps.
> *"no le vamos a querer mandar el mail 3 porque ya sabemos que esta persona... ya hizo lo que necesitábamos"*
