# Lila sync — 2026-09-25

## TLDR

A ~62-minute one-on-one. Most of it was Gaston teaching Lila how Smartlead inboxes work, to close out
her Smartlead onboarding. It ended with two jobs handed to her. **Wins first.** This week set a
**14-booked-call record**, the best since Dec 2025. Gaston counts **41 month-to-date** (26 before this
week plus 15 this week) against a **48** goal, so **7 more are needed Mon–Wed** (last Mon–Wed produced
~10). He credits fast reply times, the follow-up subsequences and the copy changes. **Inbox model.**
There are **507 sender accounts** across three providers. **Premium Inboxes** puts 3 inboxes on each
domain and rotates whole domains between group **A** and group **B** every ~2 months, so the resting
group recovers its reputation (167 inboxes are tagged B). **Cheap Inboxes** (tag Near CI) puts 1 inbox on
each domain, never rotates, and sits in both the A and B campaigns. **Internally Managed** is legacy and
no longer used. Inboxes tagged **Do not use** belong to the sales team. The standard settings are
**15 sends/day**, a **17-min gap**, and warm-up of **8/day at a 60% reply rate**. **Health ladder.** When
warm-up reputation drops, the daily limit is cut, then raised again in steps (1 → 3 → 5 → 10 → 15) as it
recovers. Gaston is extra cautious with inboxes climbing back from the bottom. At the time of the call,
**19 inboxes were at 0–70%** and **13 at 71–80%**. Gaston dropped 3 of them to 1/day during the call.
Because this has all been manual, he once missed a week and found **65%-reputation inboxes still sending
15/day**. **Handoffs to Lila:** (1) clean up the tags (fold Near 2 / Near 3 CI into Near CI, strip the
legacy AI Engineering Service tag, then delete the dead tags with Gaston). (2) Use `/skill-creator` to
build a Claude Code skill. It pulls inbox warm-up data from the Smartlead API, stores a history,
compares each run with the last one, and recommends daily-limit changes while skipping `Do not use`
inboxes. **A human approves every change.** It runs manually about every 2 days, and may become a
routine once its advice is reliable.

## Context

- **Why this matters:** *"si mandamos el mail y ese mail no le llega a nuestro lead... todo lo anterior no
  sirve para nada"* (if the email never reaches the lead, everything before it is worthless).
  [@21:29](https://fathom.video/calls/837061087?timestamp=1289)
- **A/B rotation:** B campaigns (for example *Open Jobs from Current Clients Contacts… - B Inboxes*)
  exclude A inboxes, and A campaigns exclude B. Near CI inboxes sit in both. An A-group duplicate of the
  current campaign is expected within ~1–2 months. [@17:48](https://fathom.video/calls/837061087?timestamp=1068)
- **Where the Cheap Inboxes model came from:** a cold-email agency's tip. It needs more domains
  (100 inboxes means 100 domains, versus 34 on Premium), but nothing has to be managed or rotated. So
  far it has worked. [@26:27](https://fathom.video/calls/837061087?timestamp=1587)
- **Warm-up:** Smartlead's pool sends and replies between inboxes to imitate real business mail, and
  ramps up gradually from zero. Gaston is keeping the conservative 8/day at 60% because it works.
  [@28:34](https://fathom.video/calls/837061087?timestamp=1714)
- **The ladder in practice:** inboxes at 71–72% that climbed from below stay at 1/day. They move to 3
  or 5 at about 75–77%, to 10 at 80–90%, and to 15 near 100%. An inbox that falls from 95% to 85% goes
  from 15 down to ~10. [@34:20](https://fathom.video/calls/837061087?timestamp=2060) · [@37:18](https://fathom.video/calls/837061087?timestamp=2238)
- **The CNAME walkthrough was promised but never happened.** The call ended first, so that part of the
  Smartlead onboarding is still open. [@10:19](https://fathom.video/calls/837061087?timestamp=619)
- **This follows the inbox-health analysis** of the ~30 inboxes cycling through health problems, which
  Gaston marked done this week. The skill is how that monitoring gets done from now on.
