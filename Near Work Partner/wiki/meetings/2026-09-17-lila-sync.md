# Lila sync — 2026-09-17

## TLDR

A ~36-minute one-on-one, in three parts. **(1) FounderOS.** A current client replied to a cold email.
While on the call, Gaston found the cause. Our lead (Matt Gray) carries the domain **fos.now**, which
redirects to founderos.com, but HubSpot stores the company as **founderos.com**. The current-client
exclusion searches HubSpot by company domain only, so the lookup came back empty and he got emailed.
Gaston called this rare, and said a better match (on LinkedIn page or company name) could come later.
It is **not a priority**, and it must not slow down Lila's reply times. **(2) Follow-up copy.** Gaston
approved Lila's rewritten follow-up copy (*"me gusta mucho más esta versión... saldría con esto"*, I like
this version much better, I'd go with it). The theme is selling the meeting, not Near. They made a few
edits on the call. Meeting Request step 2 becomes "in the meeting we can share a comparison of total
hiring costs in the US vs LATAM over a year". "You can book here" becomes the hyperlink itself. The sender
name is replaced with Smartlead's `%signature%`. **(3) Subsequence build.** Gaston showed the build live on
**Experiment 12** (US Open Jobs · No Hiring Manager · Sales). He created a new lead category, **Meeting Request
Follow-Up**, and a subsequence named `Sub-{campaign}-MeetingRequestFollowUp`. It triggers on that category
after a **1-day delay** and sends **5 emails, one day apart**. Settings: weekdays 09:00–18:00, every
20 min, 1,000 new leads/day, auto-pause on company-level reply off, and a **70/30 split between new leads
and follow-ups**. Then he hit a blocker. **A per-lead booking-link variable can't be a hyperlink**, so
they dropped the booking link from the subsequences for now and use a "send me two times" CTA instead.
Lila suggested a **single booking link that routes by employee count**, and Gaston will ask Paz whether
it's feasible. Next steps: Lila finishes all the subsequences in Exp 12. Then she and Gaston use **Claude
Code** to copy them to every Open Jobs campaign.

## Context

- **Current-client exclusion rule, as stated.** We only contact a company if it is not a client, has no
  open deal, and isn't in an active process. Older companies that are already in HubSpot are fine.
  Gaston named two known ways the rule fails. One is a domain mismatch between the lead and HubSpot
  (FounderOS). The other is when the CEO of the company we're prospecting is also a founder at an
  existing client.
- **Naming and trigger mechanics.** Subsequences are named `Sub-{campaign name}-{subsequence}`. Each
  one fires on a Smartlead **lead category**, and categories are created under Settings → Lead
  Categories. The existing CV-sent one is `Candidate Sent Follow-Up`. A subsequence stays in **Draft**
  until it has a start date in its settings.
- **Test sends and previews need a lead in the subsequence.** Gaston couldn't send a test email on
  the call because none was loaded.
- **Why a booking-link variable fails.** Smartlead can hyperlink a fixed URL, but not a per-lead
  variable. Our AE links depend on company size, so today each lead's link is a variable. Lila's idea
  was one link that asks for employee count and assigns the AE. If a 50+ lead finds no time with Pedro,
  the "let me know other times" line catches it and we move them to another AE manually.
- **Relation to earlier plans.** This is the "align this week, execute next week" step on the CV-sent
  subsequence planned in the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-14-lila-first-week-check|Sep 14 check]]. The call
  shows the scope grew: there is now a **Meeting Request** follow-up subsequence alongside CV-sent.
- **Diarization is poor.** Many of Gaston's explanations are attributed to Lila (@17:11, @29:55,
  @32:51, @35:22). Owners above come from what was said, not from the speaker labels.
