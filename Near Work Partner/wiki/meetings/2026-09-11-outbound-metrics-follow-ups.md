# Outbound Metrics + Follow Ups — The Follow-Up Motion Handoff

## TLDR
- **The headline is the attribution data.** Of ~10–12 calls booked per week, roughly **60% same-week
  and 40% from previous-week follow-ups**. **Last week collapsed to 92% same-week — 1 booking from
  follow-ups versus 5 the week before** — and Gaston attributes the miss against the 15-call target
  directly to weak follow-up execution.
- **No manual follow-ups were done at all this week** (week of Sep 8), because Gaston hadn't yet
  trained Lila. This is a gap, stated outright, not a completion.
- **The automated CV-Sent subsequence gets rebuilt**: from a flat 3 emails at day 1/3/5 into **~5
  direct call-booking touches over ~7–10 days, then ~3 softer nurture touches every 15 days.**
  Lila owns the copy proposal; only Gaston can put it live in Smartlead.
- **Manual follow-ups get structure**: Tue/Thu blocks (1–2h) from the week of Sep 14, working exactly
  three Smartlead statuses, capped at ~3 touches.

## Key Decisions
- **Manual follow-up target segment is exactly three statuses:** Interested, Meeting Request,
  Information Request. **Excluded by design:** Meeting Booked (converted), CV Sent where the lead
  declined (lost), and Not Qualified (intentionally closed).
- **Only leads whose last interaction was 2+ days ago** — same-day and next-day replies are left alone.
- **Manual follow-ups cap at ~3 touches**, unlike automated, because a larger manual pipeline makes
  5–7 hand-written touches unmanageable.
- **Follow-up 3 may drop the direct call ask** in favour of an open-ended question — people reply
  more readily when nothing is being asked of them. Lila is free to propose otherwise.
- **An incoming positive reply interrupts the follow-up block** and is answered immediately, to
  protect the response-time KPI.
- **Copy gets drafted through a dedicated ChatGPT Project** ("AI Copywriter Follow-Ups") fed the
  prior thread and our last reply, **with human review before sending**.
- Gaston explicitly gave Lila latitude to redesign: *"Nada de esto es tipo fijo y estructurado."*

## Proposed Weekly Cadence (Gaston's draft, admitted not fully clear)
- **Tuesday** — follow-up #1 to interested leads from last Thu/Fri, plus #2/#3 to older leads already
  followed up without reply.
- **Thursday** — follow-up #1 to leads from Mon/Tue/Wed, plus #2/#3 to prior-week leads.

- This week: **5 booked, 8 had (4 qualified / 4 unqualified), 2 roles sourced.** Target is **15/week**;
  the team currently runs 10–14.
