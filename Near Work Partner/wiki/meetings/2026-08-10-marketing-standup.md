# Marketing Stand Up — Six Calls Escalation

## TLDR
- **Andres pressed hard on only 6 calls booked from email the prior week.** Gaston confirmed
  sending, deliverability and Clay workflows all ran correctly, so the drop is likely
  segmentation-related — underperforming experiment arms accounted for ~19,000 contacted leads.
- **Gaston and Daniel committed to running the BigQuery role/persona and full-funnel analysis the
  same day**, so the numbers were ready for the next day's meeting.
- **Clay credits were down to ~50,000 or less** — the purchase that should have happened the prior
  week hadn't. 600k to buy immediately, another 600k next month (can't buy it all in one month),
  bridging to the December annual renewal.
- Andres also demanded a per-lead and per-phone-number credit cost breakdown so cost per qualified
  call can be recalculated, and that credit optimisation become a **formal** recurring process
  rather than ad hoc.

  segmentation and distribution across experiment arms.
- Ivan Valenzuela joins the outbound team as cold caller Wed 2026-08-12, welcomed at Thursday's
  standup (no Wednesday standup).
