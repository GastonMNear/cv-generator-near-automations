## Context

- **Pick-up delay on calls.** A prospect asked Ivan whether he was on a dialer after ~15 seconds of
  silence. Daniel attributed it to parallel dialing plus Ivan reading the lead context. Gaston's view
  is that the dialer should connect instantly. Ivan says he often can't tell when a call has already
  dropped to voicemail. That is why he tags some calls as "silent."
- **Opening problem.** The first qualifying question ("are you the person handling hiring?") gets an
  immediate "no" and a hang-up from CEOs. Daniel wants more positive conversations: *"as soon as you
  have a positive conversation... the rate is pretty good."*
- **Lead freshness** was named the other lever. Daniel called it hard to improve without warming
  leads in a CRM, so it is parked for now.
- **Parallel dialing for form submissions.** Ivan asked about 3–6 lines instead of 10. Gaston: *"We
  can't do them with this dialer."* Revisit on CallTools.
- **Manual follow-ups (not CV-sent).** Gaston suggested going from 3 to 5 touches and grouping
  interested replies that ask the same thing (for example, process questions) into their own
  subsequence, modeled on CV-sent.
- **RB2B retargeting list.** It came up as "never uploaded" to the dialer. The transcript doesn't make
  clear who was meant to upload it.
