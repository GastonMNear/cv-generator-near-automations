# Outbound Volume Session — 2026-10-13

*(Fathom title: "Outbound volume session". Recorded by Carlos Barrera. In English. Hayden, Robyn and Will attended but did not speak. Deck: "10-13_Near_Weekly_Outbound_Volume_Session".)*

## TLDR
- **The huddle goes bi-weekly, with a weekly written update in between.** Andrés proposed it because one week is rarely enough data [@35:24](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=2124). Gaston, Felix, Franco and Michael agreed. Franco's condition: on off-weeks, send an update with the **headlines, the metrics and how they changed, and an explanation of any meaningful change** (e.g. a spam warning) [@38:43](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=2323). Gaston: "Okay, sounds good." The next session date was not stated. Every other Tuesday would make it Oct 27.
- **16 calls booked:** 10 Email, 6 LinkedIn, 0 Cold Calling [@01:46](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=106). Email positive replies recovered to **60**, and reply rate rose to **3.97%**. Emails sent were ~18K, stuck in the 18–21K range for ~2 months.
- **The volume blocker is the supply of quality leads, not inboxes** [@15:24](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=924). Inbox reputation limits cost ~2,000 leads/wk. Fully maxed out, the inboxes would send ~23K, so the gap is leads. Open-job volume is flat on average. The lead base shrank because qualification got tighter (roles, and excluding companies over 500 employees) [@18:58](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=1138). Andrés: "the solve is really to find new sources of open jobs" [@20:14](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=1214).
- **HireBase comes first, ahead of funding-signal campaigns.** Andrés, Franco and Michael all endorsed it. Funding signals are crowded ("everyone is using that as a signal"). Michael's principle: unique data sources beat what everyone pulls from Clay and Apollo [@24:52](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=1492). Gaston's plan: get a meeting with a HireBase AE to ask for a larger free trial. If that fails, use the free tier (500 job openings/month) to measure overlap with current sources, then decide on a plan [@22:20](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=1340).
- **Apollo as a precedent:** about **1 call/wk on ~4K emails/wk**. That is "not on par with the baseline" but good enough to justify testing new sources [@23:50](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=1430).
- **Cold calling (Carlos):** dials were up 65% to 5.5K, but there were only 2 positive conversations from 141 human pickups. Cutting lines from 10 to 5 lowered hang-ups on real answerers from 18% to 10%. 9 of 10 numbers show as spam on Felix's Canadian phone. Next: US numbers from big-city area codes, with pickup measured against the 2.3% baseline [@26:25](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=1585). Ivan says most pickups have open roles that are not remote. Andrés wants a breakdown of the non-positive pickup reasons by percentage [@29:42](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=1782).
- **LinkedIn (Felix):** Sam is back on pace. The capacity opener on the Andrés account got 28% accept and 5% reply. The Pedro ("Petro") account got 20% accept, 6% reply and 2 positives. The new finance-leaders outreach on Sam's account will lead with **motive #1, affordable US-quality talent with the right knowledge** [@10:10](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=610).

## Key Decisions
- **Outbound Volume Session cadence → bi-weekly**, plus a **weekly written update** (headlines + metrics + w/w change + why) in the off-weeks [@38:43](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=2323). This changes the `weekly-volume-session` process.
- **Test HireBase as a new open-jobs source** before brainstorming non-open-job campaigns such as funding signals [@21:25](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=1285).
- **Finance-leader LinkedIn opener = affordability-first (motive #1).** Andrés and Franco suggested a variant: when the open role is finance/accounting-specific, add knowledge/tools; otherwise lead with cost savings. Felix settled on #1 to test first.
- **No weekly copywriting workshop for now.** Gaston: wait until Paz and Alexia are onboarded on Clay [@37:20](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=2240). Andrés restated the rule that **only ~30% of capacity is used for tests and 70% is protected for what already works** [@37:57](https://fathom.video/calls/858323233?timestamp=2277).
