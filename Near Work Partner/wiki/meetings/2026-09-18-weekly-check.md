# Weekly check — 2026-09-18

## TLDR

Friday outbound review, dominated by a **lead-volume shortfall**. Last week booked **7 calls** (it
"should have been 13"). Of last week's **66 positive replies**, only ~10 converted. Lila reports the
remaining ~56 are mostly ghosting, flat no's or out-of-office, even after 3–4 follow-ups each. This week
had **46 positives** at call time (heading for 48–50, against a normal ~50–55). Median reply time
improved to **~9 min**. The root problem is volume: roughly **2,000 fewer new leads over 4 weeks**, and
Gaston has confirmed (with AI analysis) that **opportunities found dropped ~20% since Experiment 12
launched**. The drop hits every segment, "Others" included. There are two hypotheses. Either the
market softened by coincidence, or the setup (three keyword-specific workflows plus a generic one that
**excludes** the sales/marketing/engineering keywords) is over-excluding. Daniel's strategic read: if
segmenting loses 20%, the performance gain can't recover it, **so segment expansion is on hold**. Gaston
emails Clay support and the investigation becomes **top priority, pushing back the cold-call/voicemail
follow-up work**. **Current clients** get re-approached by **cold calling only** (Ivan presenting
himself as the SDR). Email stays shelved after a client complained to Hayden. This needs alignment with
Carlos (sales) on the 3-month no-contact rule. Also agreed: a one-off **"Weekend Follow Up"**
Sat/Sun blast to all interested leads from the last 2 weeks, plus the 5-day follow-up subsequences
going live Monday. Ivan is at **3,538 dials** (target 4,000; he lost half a day to form follow-ups)
and **~3–4 bookings** this week.
