# Lila Onboarding Sync — Cold Email Engine Walkthrough

> [!warning] **Diarization drifted on this recording.** Several speaker labels are swapped (the

> Ownership below is grounded in content, not labels.

## TLDR
- First onboarding session with **Lila Lemus**, who takes over email reply handling. Gaston walked
  the whole cold email engine, the HubSpot pipeline, the business model and the team structure.
- **Her scope starts deliberately narrow: positive replies only.** Intermediate and negative replies
  stay *unanswered* for capacity reasons — acknowledged out loud as suboptimal, not as a policy win.
- **Training method: watch-then-reverse.** Gaston answers positive replies live with Lila watching,
  then she answers with him watching. This is the concrete ramp mechanism the Sep 4 handoff task lacked.
- **Reporting line clarified:** Lila → Gaston (day-to-day) → Daniel Ardila (Outbound) → Andres
  Bahamon (VP Marketing, over both inbound and outbound).
- **The unanswered-reply bucket is quantified upside:** non-positives outnumber positives ~4:1, and
  recovering one extra booking/week off a ~10/week baseline is ~10% growth.

## Key Decisions
- **Positive replies only, for now.** Intermediate replies (*"already working with another recruiter"*,
  *"no LATAM employees"*) are a known untapped bucket, explicitly deferred rather than dropped.
- **AI-drafted replies for the non-positive bucket are medium-term** — agent drafts, human presses
  send. Deferred until Lila has ramped.
- **Booking-link discipline:** always use Cold Email's own booking links so HubSpot attributes the
  pre-sales deal to **Email Outreach**.
- **Qualification bias: when in doubt, book it.** A missed qualified call costs more than an
  unqualified one. Hard exclusions are staffing/recruiting companies and roles requiring US
  citizenship or on-site presence.
- **Team KPI is qualified booked calls**, not revenue — downstream is outside Cold Email's control.
- **Recurring non-agenda 1:1** added at Lila's request; Gaston accepted.

## Business Context Captured
- **Reply volume:** ~20 positive replies/day, ~5 min each once ramped. Baseline ~10 qualified
  calls/week.
- **HubSpot deal flow:** `PS-<Company>` pre-sales (Call Scheduled / Cancelled / Qualified /
  Unqualified) → Sales (`<Company>`) → Fulfillment (`<Company>-<Role>`) → Placements.
- **Business model:** ~30% recruiting (one-time fee, client runs payroll) / ~70% staffing (monthly
  fee, Near handles payroll, legality, offboarding). **No upfront fees** — only on placement.
- **Channel mix:** Open Jobs is ~90% of send volume and the best-converting branch; retargeting
  (web visitors) second; one-shot campaigns have no consistent formula yet.
- **Apollo Open Jobs** launched ~2–3 weeks prior as an experiment: Apollo supplies companies with
  open roles, Clay runs two job-board scrapers plus an AI fallback that browses the company's own
  `/jobs` or `/careers` page.
- **The Guarantors** is the best client ever sourced from Cold Email — 97 associated deals.
