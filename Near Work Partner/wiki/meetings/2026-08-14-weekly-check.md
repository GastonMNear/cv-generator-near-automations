# Weekly Check — Outbound Channel Review

## TLDR
- **New weekly reporting process:** each channel owner sends Daniel a qualitative slide (context,
  experiments, learnings, next week) by Monday morning, plus daily quantitative metrics in Gaston's
  sheet. Daniel assembles these into Tuesday's leadership presentation. Gaston owns email;
  Sam/Mairol own LinkedIn; Ivan owns cold calling.
- **The LinkedIn lead list is the week's top priority.** Two weeks overdue to Felix; Daniel
  escalated it to "prioridad 000000001" for Monday and is deliberately keeping it *out* of the
  leadership deck rather than show a dragging task on the best-converting channel.
- **Deliverability is a prime suspect for August's email underperformance.** 50 Smartlead inbox
  tests launched for the weekend; no historical baseline exists to compare against.
- **Weak week overall:** 6-7 calls booked on email, 1 on LinkedIn, 1 on cold calling (Ivan's first
  day, from 60 dials / 3 conversations). Positive replies were strong (~8 in one afternoon).
- **LinkedIn has hit a targeting ceiling** — the actively-hiring-CEO pool in assigned states is
  exhausted weekly, and Felix's rule against contacting non-CEO titles blocks the obvious workaround.

## Key Decisions
- Weekly reporting standardised: qualitative slide + daily metrics, delivered Monday morning.
- Gaston covers LinkedIn slides and headlines this week (Sam is out); Daniel is OOO Monday.
- Daniel calls each channel owner Tuesday ~08:00 COT to assemble the deck before the midday
  leadership meeting.
- **Weekend campaign to be paused** so it doesn't contaminate the deliverability tests — it also
  produces almost no replies and had high bounces.
- LinkedIn lead list ships Monday after the slides; recipients will run the existing Codex/Claude
  Code skill to correct missing fields on low/medium-ranked leads (~10% lead recovery).
- Ivan's offer-letter KPIs are outdated — parallel dialing changes the targets to ~40 conversations
  per day rather than 200 dials.

the broadened audiences within the No-Hiring-Manager campaigns, which underperformed by roughly 30%
while consuming a large lead volume. Open Jobs campaign analysis is complete with an action plan.

follow-ups and deliverability work, and will run next week.

Mairol's LinkedIn constraint is structural: she exhausts the actively-hiring pool in her assigned
states each week, and Felix instructed her not to contact non-CEO/President titles even when the
job post is a clear fit. Daniel disputes that rule — email converts on the same niche — and sees
the lead list as the resolution, since it identifies the actual hiring manager per job post.
