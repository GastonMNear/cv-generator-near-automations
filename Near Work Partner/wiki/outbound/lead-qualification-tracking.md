# Lead qualification → booking tracking

Lila runs a qualification skill on positive replies before pushing for a call, to raise the qualified-call rate.
Gaston asked her to report how many leads land in **Unqualified** or **Semi-Qualified** and how many of those
still book. The question is how many potential calls the filter leaves on the table.
Each report gets a new dated section below. Older sections stay as they are.

## Report 1: Oct 9–29, 2026 (from Lila, received Oct 30)

- **24 leads tracked.** 20 of them were Semi-Qualified or Unqualified.
- **Only 1 of those 20 booked a meeting:** Iluma (Semi-Qualified). The company's origin, which is what
  made it Semi, is still unconfirmed.
- **No Unqualified lead booked.**
- **Cindy (ButcherJoseph):** the company confirmed what the audit flagged. They don't accept remote.
  The skill called this one correctly.

**Reading (Gaston, Oct 30):** we are leaving a lot of calls on the table. 19 of the 20 Semi/Unqualified
opportunities were lost, and the current handling (sending a CV) doesn't recover them. These leads need a
different play to get them to a call.
