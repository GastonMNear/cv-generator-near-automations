# Restructure the automated follow-up sequence sent after a CV

The subsequence fires when a lead is tagged **CV Sent** in Smartlead. Today it is **3 emails at
roughly day 1, 3 and 5**, all referencing the profile already sent. Replace with:

- **~5 direct, call-booking-oriented follow-ups** within the first ~7–10 days
- **then ~3 softer nurture touches every 15 days**, framed as *"maybe you already filled this role,
  but are there other opportunities we can help with?"*

**Why this is high priority — the numbers make the case.** Of ~10–12 calls booked weekly, **~40% come
from previous-week follow-ups**. Last week that collapsed: **92% same-week, only 1 booking from
follow-ups versus 5 the week before**, and Gaston ties the miss against the 15-call target directly
to follow-up execution. This copy is revenue-driving, and Gaston admits he *"armé hace un tiempo, que
la verdad que nunca más tuve tiempo de revisarlo ni de optimizarlo."*

**His hypothesis:** *"si una persona mostró interés, es de los leads más relevantes que tenemos para
trabajar, entonces ¿por qué le mandaríamos solo tres?"*

**Split of ownership:**
- **Lila** writes the copy proposal (per-step copy for the new cadence) — see Waiting on.
- **Gaston** makes the structural change in Smartlead (step count, 15-day spacing) and applies the
  approved copy. Only he can push it live.

**Personalisation variables available** and expected in the generic follow-ups: first name, role being
hired for, company industry.

## Update 2026-09-11 (evening) — Daniel confirmed and sharpened the shape

Raised again in the **weekly check** the same day. Daniel independently agreed and pushed it **harder
than the morning version**: not ~5 touches over 7–10 days but **five consecutive days** — *"cinco días
seguidos, pum, pum, pum, pum, pum"* — with differentiated messages each day, then the spaced nurture
touches.

**Daniel's four sketched angles** for the daily follow-ups (nothing finalised):
1. what happened / did you see it
2. is the role still open
3. do you want me to proceed
4. just say yes and I'll tell the team

**The nurture touches should ask about *new* openings, not the original role** — identifiable from
Clay — since the original vacancy may already be filled.

**The numbers Daniel used:** ~60 positive replies → only ~10 book. Of the remaining ~50, **30–40
receive CVs** and then go quiet. His framing: these already said yes, so the team is *"leaving money
on the table"* — he wants **two supremely intensive weeks** per opportunity before backing off.

**Confirmed as next week's priority** by Gaston: *"seguramente sea prioridad la semana que viene...
eso prioridad para la semana que viene también."* Still jointly designed with Lila.
