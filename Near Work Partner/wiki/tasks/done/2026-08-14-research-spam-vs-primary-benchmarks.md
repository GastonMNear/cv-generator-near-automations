# Research spam vs primary benchmarks

Find the industry benchmark for what share of cold email should land in primary vs spam, so the
Smartlead test results can be judged against something.

**Why:** The tests produce a number (say 25% spam) that means nothing alone — this was never
measured before, so there is no baseline. Without a market benchmark nobody can tell a crisis from
normal. Needed for Monday's email slides so leadership sees it in context. Done = figures sourced
and in the slide.

> el benchmark."
