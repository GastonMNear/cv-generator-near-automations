# There is no record of which follow-up number each lead has received

The manual follow-up process has **no stage tracking at all**. Lila has to reconstruct where each lead
stands by reading every Master Inbox thread individually.

**Why:** without it the Tue/Thu follow-up blocks scale badly — the reading overhead grows with the
pipeline, and leads get skipped or double-touched. Manual follow-ups are capped at ~3 touches, so
knowing which touch a lead is on is the whole control mechanism.

**Path:** Lila is researching whether follow-up tracking software exists for this industry — her prior
system assigned a likelihood score per contact by stage. **If nothing affordable turns up, Gaston
builds a custom dashboard.** So this task is gated on her research (see Waiting on).

**Timing:** No date. Verbatim: *"seguramente sea una tarea que tomemos en las próximas semanas para
darle un poco más de estructura a todo el tema este de follow-ups."*
