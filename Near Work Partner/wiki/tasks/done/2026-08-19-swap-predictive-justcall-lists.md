# Swap the Predictive campaign lists in place

Pull the full current list out of the two Predictive (parallel-dialing) JustCall campaigns and load
the new one in. Names stay the same, so no reconfiguration is needed.

**Why:** These two campaigns **cannot be archived** — parallel/power dialing only works on
pre-existing campaigns, a known issue from the Ramón setup, previously solved the same way by
refreshing the lists rather than rebuilding. Everything else gets archived; these two get swapped.
Done = old lists out, new lists in, both campaigns still dialing.

**Timing:** same week as the lead upload; no specific day given.

> "Esas no las puedes archivar porque gasto lo que va a hacer es sacar la lista completa que está
> ahí y poner una nueva."
