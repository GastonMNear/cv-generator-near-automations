# Find out whether PhoneBurner lifts the reattempt ceiling

Contact PhoneBurner sales and confirm whether their plan supports **parallel dialing** and **20
reattempts**, and at which tier. Pricing seen: **$140/user on the annual plan, $165/user monthly**.
Also confirm whether **a single seat is enough**, given JustCall's two-dialer limit is what constrains
us today.

**Why:** **JustCall caps reattempts at 4 where the team wants 20.** That exhausts lists too fast and
leaves valid numbers uncalled — Ivan's evidence from Aug 24 is that a lead converted on **attempt 4**
while claiming never to have been contacted. A dialer allowing 20 attempts is the **structural fix**,
not a workaround.

Andres to approve or reject.
