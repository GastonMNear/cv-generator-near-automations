# Both day-one positive replies asked for CVs instead of routing us to the right person

The new Engineering/Marketing/Sales No-HM campaigns produced two positive replies on day one. **Both
asked for CVs rather than routing to the right person — which was the campaigns' actual objective.**
It was noticed and then dropped.

> Gaston: "ambas nos pidieron [CVs], o sea, no nos rutearon" — Daniel: "curioso, porque ahora el
> objetivo era el routing"

**Why this is worth a look:** the whole premise of the segment copy is that a non-hiring-manager will
**forward or route** the email to whoever owns the requisition. Two for two asking for CVs instead is a
small sample, but it points the other way — the recipients engaged **as buyers**, not as routers. If
that holds, the routing hypothesis is wrong and the copy objective should change.

**It also cuts against the segmentation debate.** If recipients respond as buyers regardless of the
role being hired, that supports segmenting by **recipient title** rather than role
([[wiki/tasks/2026-09-02-resolve-roles-vs-titles-segmentation]]) — and it echoes the standing finding
that the CEO at a small company is the economic buyer whatever the open role is.

**Done =** enough replies accumulated to say whether routing happens at all, and a decision on whether
the campaigns' objective stays "get routed" or becomes "sell directly."
