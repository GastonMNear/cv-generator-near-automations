# Launch the new No-HM motion — segment-specific copywriting

Ship the reframed No-HM motion as **three function-specific copy variants**: engineering, sales, and
marketing. Requested directly by Gaston 2026-08-27.

**Why now:** the strategy behind this is already approved and closed —
[[wiki/tasks/done/2026-08-24-review-conversion-improvement-strategy]] (Daniel's priority 1) reviewed
the reframed copy and settled the test bed, but **nothing open tracked actually launching it**. The
thinking is done; this is the execution half.

## The reframe this implements
The baseline underperforms in No-HM because the copy **assumes the recipient is the hiring manager** —
in No-HM that's a coin flip at only ~1.4 people reached per company. The fix is to stop presuming and
**ask to be routed to whoever owns the process**:

acknowledge they're hiring → mention the teammates also contacted → job-description snippet to prove
relevance → offer candidate profiles → **ask to be pointed at the process owner**.

## Scope — exactly these three segments
Engineering, sales, and marketing. This was Gaston's argument and Daniel agreed: thousands of leads,
known-poor performance, so pulling them into a test costs **at most one booked call** while still
giving enough volume for significance.

> Expand to the other 47 of Daniel's **50 underperforming segments** only once these three prove out.

## Guardrails — both agreed with Daniel, do not violate
- **Do not experiment on hiring managers.** The baseline works on them regardless of function.
- **Do not experiment on No-HM CEOs/founders under 50.** Also converts. Testing either would damage
  the only rates currently working.
- **Drop the teammate-mention line when only one contact exists** at a company — it breaks otherwise.
  Daniel accepted this.

## What "function-specific" has to earn
Per-segment copy is only worth the operational cost if the *segment* changes the message, not just a
job title token. For each of engineering / sales / marketing, the JD snippet and the offered profiles
should differ in substance. If a variant ends up being the generic copy with a word swapped, collapse
it back into one and say so — three arms that are secretly one arm split the volume and buy nothing.

## Open question to resolve at launch
This tests **what** we write. [[wiki/tasks/2026-08-25-revisit-decision-making-unit]] argues the
problem is partly **who** we write to — HR owns hiring in companies over ~50, and HR/Talent is only
8.2% of No-HM leads. Both can be true. Decide deliberately whether this motion holds targeting
constant (cleaner read on copy) or moves both at once (faster, confounded). **Holding targeting
constant is the safer call** unless Daniel wants speed over attribution.

## Done =
Three segment-specific arms live in Smartlead on No-HM engineering / sales / marketing, leads loaded,
volume caps respected, HM and sub-50 CEO/founder segments untouched, and the arm structure on the
report matching what is actually running.

**Note:** if these arms need weekly lead top-ups, they inherit the manual cost Daniel objected to —
see [[wiki/tasks/2026-08-26-automate-experiment-lead-adds]] rather than hand-adding again.
