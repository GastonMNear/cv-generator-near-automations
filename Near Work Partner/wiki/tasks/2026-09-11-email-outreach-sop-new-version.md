# New version of the Email Outreach SOP

Daniel asked for a **new version** of the Email Outreach SOP. In progress.

**Why now:** the process it documents has changed substantially in the last two weeks, and much of
what changed currently lives only in meeting notes and in Gaston's head:

- **Reply handling changed owner** — Hector → Lila, with scope deliberately narrowed to **positive
  replies only**; intermediate and negative replies go unanswered by design.
- **The follow-up motion was restructured** — manual follow-ups on Tue/Thu blocks against three
  Smartlead statuses (Interested / Meeting Request / Information Request), leads 2+ days stale, ~3
  touches max; the automated CV-Sent subsequence is being rebuilt 3 → ~5 direct + ~3 nurture.
- **The CV generation project now reads from Smartlead, not Clay tables.**
- **Reply-speed KPI defined:** share of replies under 10 minutes, business hours only.
- **Untracked Replies** has a daily review procedure and a manual association fix.
- **AE booking links route by company size**, availability overriding — *still unconfirmed*, see
  [[wiki/tasks/2026-09-09-send-lila-ae-booking-link-table]].

**Worth folding in while writing it:** several operating rules surfaced in the Sep 8–11 sessions are
currently tribal knowledge with no written home — the Smartlead-registered-email lookup key, the
sample-CV framing rules, the persona/inbox signature match, and the Clay remote-filter false-positive
workaround. An SOP is the natural place for them.
