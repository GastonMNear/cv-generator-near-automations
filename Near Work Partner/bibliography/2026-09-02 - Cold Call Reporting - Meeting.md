76-minute session that redesigned the cold-call funnel to mirror the email funnel (Dials → Connect
Rate → Machine/Human Pickups → Positive Conversations → Booked), with Positive Conversation Rate
computed over Human Pickups rather than Dials. Reporting was deliberately kept manual because a
dialer change is expected within weeks. Also settled the re-engagement subsequence design — including
a 1–2 week recency window that supersedes the vault's earlier 2–3 month assumption — and the No-HM
routing rule protecting CEOs under 50 in Sales/Marketing while sending all engineering to the new copy.
An unexplained voicemail gap (~3,269 machine pickups vs ~301 voicemails left) is the largest open item.
