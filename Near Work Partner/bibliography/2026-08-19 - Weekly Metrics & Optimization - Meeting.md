Outbound team weekly, dominated by a ground-up rebuild of cold calling. Dial lists stop being
segmented by whether an email was found; all hiring managers go into one parallel-dialing list
prioritised by hiring role and title, with attempt cadence raised from 4 to ~20 per lead over two
weeks. Gaston owns lead loading, Ivan takes over JustCall configuration. On email, candidate-lead
is retired in favour of routing as the single No-HM baseline, Experiment 9's baseline arm dropped.
BigQuery remains broken on a billing problem Gaston cannot fix himself.
