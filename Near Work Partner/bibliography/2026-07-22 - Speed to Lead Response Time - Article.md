Speed-to-lead research aggregated by YetiConnect (a HubSpot/SMS sales-automation vendor — treat the "sub-60-second" product claims as marketing, the cited studies as solid). Underpins the [[wiki/context/reply-methodology-playbook]].

Key data used:
- Avg B2B first-response time = **42 hours** (HBR, 2,241 companies); 23% never respond; only **7%** respond within 5 min.
- Respond within **5 min → 21x** more likely to qualify vs. 30 min (MIT/InsideSales). After 5 min, qualifying odds drop **80%**; after 10 min, odds of reaching the lead drop **400%**.
- Respond within **1 min → +391%** conversion vs. 2 min.
- **<5 min → 100x** more likely to connect than waiting an hour (Drift); **58%** of buyers say response time is a critical vendor-selection factor.
- **First vendor into a real conversation wins 35–50%** of competitive deals — sets the evaluation criteria.
- Measurement: track avg time-to-first-response, response-time distribution, conversion by response-time bucket, per-rep speed; visible wallboards improve avg response time ~35%.

Applied to Near: a positive reply to a cold email = the peak-intent moment (our analog to an inbound form fill), so the same urgency logic drives our two-email reply flow.
