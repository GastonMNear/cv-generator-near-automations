Factor-by-factor diagnosis of why booked calls flatlined at 30–46/month all year despite roughly
doubling sending capacity since December. Deliverability largely ruled out: healthy bounce rates,
blacklisted domains performing the same as clean ones, and a live 10-inbox spam test showing zero
spam placement. The real signal is segment composition — hiring managers held at ~3.3–3.6% positive
while non-hiring managers collapsed from 1.46% to 0.53% as that segment grew from ~7k to ~17k leads.
Strategic conclusion: the baseline message has hit its ceiling; gains must come from niche
segmentation and hyper-personalised copy, not more volume.
