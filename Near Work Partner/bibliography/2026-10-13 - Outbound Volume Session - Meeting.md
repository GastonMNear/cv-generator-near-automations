Tuesday outbound huddle, presented by Gaston and recorded by Carlos. The team booked 16 calls (10 email, 6 LinkedIn, 0 cold calling). Email sends are stuck at 18–21K a week, and Gaston diagnosed the cause as a shortage of quality leads after tighter qualification, not inbox capacity. The group agreed to test HireBase as a new open-jobs source, starting with an extended or free trial to measure overlap. Cold calling is moving to US big-city numbers, and Andrés asked for a breakdown of the reasons behind pickups. The huddle moves to bi-weekly, with a weekly written update of headlines and metrics in between.
